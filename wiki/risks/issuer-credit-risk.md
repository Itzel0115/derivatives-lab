# 發行機構信用風險（Issuer Credit Risk）

> 結構型債券是發行機構的無擔保債務。不管條款寫得多好，包括「保本」在內，所有付款承諾都只跟發行機構的財務狀況一樣可靠。

**提醒**：本頁是教學說明，**不構成投資建議**。

## 重點主張

- `[事實]` 結構型債券是發行機構的無擔保債務。（S6, §Credit risk）
  > "Structured notes are unsecured debt obligations of the issuer"
- `[事實]` 發行機構的付款承諾，包括任何保本條款，都只跟發行機構的財務健全程度一樣可靠。（S6, §Credit risk）
  > "are only as good as the financial health of the structured note issuer"
- `[事實]` 如果發行機構違約，投資人可能損失部分或全部本金，以及其他應得的款項。（S6, §Credit risk）
  > "investors may lose some, or all, of the principal amount they invested"
- `[事實]` 所謂的「保本」，同樣受發行機構的信用風險影響。（S6, §Market risk）
  > "This principal protection is subject to the credit risk of the issuing financial institution."
- `[事實]` 結構型債券的任何報償，都取決於發行機構的信用。（S6, §Questions to Ask）
  > "any payoff on a structured note is subject to the creditworthiness of the issuer"

### 破產時會怎樣（S7）

- `[事實]` 任何保本的保證，都只跟做出承諾的那家公司的財務實力一樣可靠。（S7, §How Do These Notes Protect）
  > "is only as good as the financial strength of the company that makes that promise"
- `[事實]` 發行機構破產時，持有這些債券的投資人被視為無擔保債權人，可能幾乎拿不回原始投資。（S7, §How Do These Notes Protect）
  > "investors who hold these notes are considered unsecured creditors and might recover little, if anything, of their original investment"
- `[事實]` 買了 Lehman Brothers Holdings 發行的保本型結構型債券的投資人，就遇到了這種情況。（S7, §How Do These Notes Protect）
  > "This is what happened to investors who purchased structured notes with principal protection issued by now bankrupt Lehman Brothers Holdings."
- `[事實]` 結構型商品並不代表持有任何資產組合，而是發行機構的付款承諾。（S7, §What Are）
  > "do not represent ownership of any portfolio of assets but rather are promises to pay made by the product issuers"

### 和上市選擇權的對照

- `[事實]` 上市選擇權的買方依靠的是 OCC 的制度來確保履約，而不是某一個特定的賣方。（S1, p.6）
  > "An option holder looks to the system created by OCC's rules, rather than to any particular option writer, for performance of the option he owns."
